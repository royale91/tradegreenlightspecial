How it works

The tape, read for you

Big institutions can't hide size — when they fire, it prints. The Whale System watches every bar for those prints, measures the delta behind them, and draws the whole plan on your chart the instant it happens. Here's every piece.

01 · Whale prints

See the big money hit — live

Every time an outsized order prints, a thick line drops on your chart with the size and the net delta behind it. Green when buyers are lifting, red when sellers are hitting. You see who's actually in control, not what the candle color says.

  • WHALE 240 · Δ +924 @ VWAP — a buy whale, 924 net contracts to the upside
  • WHALE 2344 · Δ −2579 — a sell whale dumping into the bid

02 · Absorbed

Know who got trapped

The most important tell on the tape: a big order fires and price doesn't move. That means the other side ate it. "Sellers absorbed" means sellers hit the bid and buyers soaked it all up — fuel for a move up. The system flags it the second it happens.

  • Sellers absorbed — sell pressure got eaten → look up
  • Buyers absorbed — buy pressure got eaten → look down
  • Catches the trap before the reversal, not after
